VR Boiler Training Simulation

December 2025

Description

A VR training simulation for boiler operation and safety procedures. A scenerio based on a real life accident where the operator has to put one boiler into working condition, followed by taking the other boiler offline, while following proper safety protocols. Designed to provide an immersive learning experience for students and professionals in a controlled virtual environment. Built in Unity.

The simulation includes interactive elements, realistic 3D models, and a guided tutorial to ensure users understand the correct procedures for operating and maintaining boilers safely.

The project was developed as part of a school assignment, with a focus on creating an engaging and educational VR experience that emphasizes safety and operational best practices.
